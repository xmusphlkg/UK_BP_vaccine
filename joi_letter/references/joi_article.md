# Target article

Walker JL, Ribeiro S, Litt D, et al. Antenatal pertussis vaccine effectiveness in England remains high post COVID-19. *Journal of Infection*. 2026;93:106836. doi:10.1016/j.jinf.2026.106836.

Local source: `../PIIS0163445326001623.pdf`.

Key reported estimates used as external evidence, not re-estimated here:

- 188 laboratory-confirmed cases in infants younger than 3 months, January 2020–March 2024.
- Primary analysis: 143 infants; 31 had mothers vaccinated at least 1 week before birth.
- VE against laboratory-confirmed pertussis in infants younger than 3 months: 84.6% (95% CI 76.9–89.7%).
- VE restricted to onset before 2 months: 86.2% (95% CI 78.3–91.3%).
- No statistically significant difference by gestational age at vaccination.
- VE against hospitalisation: 88.2% (95% CI 81.0–92.7%).
- VE against death: 91.5% (95% CI 75.4–97.1%).
- VE was estimated with the screening method, using case coverage and population coverage.
- CPRD coverage dataset: 453,740 pregnancies.

The letter must not imply access to the individual-level CPRD or case-linkage data.
